learning now about stochastic discount factors. it’s a very interesting concept.

intuitively, getting $1 in a state of the world where everything is good is not as valuable as getting $1 in a state of the world where everything is bad. so, we would want to wait the $1 in the bad world more than the good world.

the stochastic discount factor is the abstract representation of this weighting. i find it quite interesting because it is making me thing about life differently. not only do the actions that we take matter, but the state of the world with which we take them matter a lot as well.

in the drake song, “firm friends”, he raps “your friends never had to decide if they wanna stay. the strength of your bond has never been tested in any way.” how valuable is a friend that is only around you in good times? how valuable is the person that checks up on you when things are poorly. we stick so much to the people in our lives that would never be there when our world is falling apart.
