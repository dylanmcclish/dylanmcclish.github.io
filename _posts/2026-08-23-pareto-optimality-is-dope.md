learning a bit of game theory for my research thesis. had a thought about pareto optimality and how it could be leveraged in our real life. basically, a game can be thought of as any scenario where two people take actions that affect themselves and each other. one can see how most situations in life can be viewed as a game.

there is a concept called nash equilibrium, which is the set of decisions that players make in a game such that they can’t individually change their decision-making to attain more optimal outcomes. thus, this can be viewed as “optimal” in some sense. however, this is not always the best outcome. nash equilibrium necesitates that the agents aren’t working together, because they are both individually changing their decisions and assume that the other agents are unchanging.

however, if we allow both agents to change together, they can learn mechanisms of cooperation that lead to better outcomes for everybody. this “most optimal” solution can be even better than the nash equilibrium solution. as i write this i realize that i don’t understand this as well i would like to. but anyways, still a cool thought.

so in life, cooperate with others — complement each other’s strengths, and you’ll both probably be better off.
