title: "unknowing"
date: 2026-11-12

it is quite strange to let go. if we focus too much on the act of letting go, we have paradoxically now given ourselves another act to let go of. it is almost as if one must step outside of themselves.

i fear that i am learning of the limits of my intellectual capacity. my neuroses seem to stem from the futile attempt to capture the world. any control that it appears i have on the world is because the world wants me to feel like i have control.

this realization has ruined me. the product of my identity is simply a means to protect myself against the world. not protect, but insulate. or ignore. but now that i have seen the darkness, i have no choice but to walk through it.

i am not sure what i will find when i give myself to the world. it’s not up to me to know.

it is quite strange. i can write these words and someone can read them, yet they will have no clue what i am talking about. no amount of intellectual understanding can amount to the resonance one feels when they have experienced.
